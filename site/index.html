How the 6th seed rule works. Six teams make the playoffs. Seeds 1–3 go to the three division winners and seeds 4–5 to the best remaining records. Seed 6 goes to the team with the most points scored among everyone left, no matter their record.

Seeds 1 and 2 get a first-round bye. Record ties are broken by points for.
